# GitPaste Code of Conduct

GitPaste is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of different viewpoints.
- Focus feedback on the work and provide enough context to make it actionable.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner's published contact or security channel.
